Vercel hires Jane Grosser as Chief Operating Officer to lead GTM

San Francisco, 14 November 2025

Vercel today announced that Jane Grosser will join the company as Chief Operating Officer, effective immediately. Grosser will lead Vercel's go-to-market organization, including sales, customer success, and revenue operations.

Grosser most recently served as Chief Revenue Officer at a leading cloud infrastructure company. In a public post, she outlined her framework for the GTM Engineer role she is now hiring at Vercel.
