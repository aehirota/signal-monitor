Retool announces $45M Series C led by Sequoia

By J. Reporter — published 2024-09-15

Retool today announced its $45 million Series C round led by Sequoia Capital, with participation from existing investors. The funding will be used to accelerate hiring and expand the company's enterprise GTM team.

Retool CEO David Hsu said the round positions the company for its next phase of growth, with new hires planned across product, engineering, and go-to-market operations over the next 12 months.

"We're focused on the enterprise segment," Hsu added in the announcement.
